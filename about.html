About SkillOra India

Bridging the career readiness gap for aspiring learners across India with accredited healthcare colleges, hospitality institutions, and direct verified employment opportunities.

Our Mission & Purpose

SkillOra is India’s premier unified education and career readiness ecosystem. Founded to solve the acute shortage of trained paramedical professionals, skilled nursing graduates, and luxury hospitality associates, SkillOra brings accredited colleges, transparent tuition structures, and genuine employer hiring desks under one trusted roof.

Every year, over 50,000 learners from tier-2 and tier-3 towns seek vocational education but face non-accredited colleges, deceptive brokerage charges, and lack of verified placement support. SkillOra eliminates intermediaries by connecting students directly with approved institutions and authorized hospital HR departments.
