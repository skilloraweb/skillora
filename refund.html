Refund & Cancellation Policy

Transparent guidelines on counseling fees, institutional seat reservation, and cancellation rights.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. 100% Free Career Guidance & Job Application

Skillora India does not charge students or job seekers any fee for browsing colleges, exploring career paths, creating profiles, or applying for hospital/hospitality job vacancies. All job applications on Skillora are 100% free.

2. Institutional Tuition & Admission Fees

When a student reserves a direct seat or completes admission at a partner college or university:

  • Course tuition fees, hostel deposits, and examination fees are paid directly to the respective recognized college or university account.
  • Refunds of tuition fees are governed directly by the respective University Grants Commission (UGC) and State Regulatory Council Refund Regulations.
  • If an admission is cancelled before the commencement of the academic batch, students are entitled to institutional refunds as mandated by national higher education norms.

3. Counseling & Document Processing Fees (Where Applicable)

If any nominal administrative or documentary verification fee is charged for special quota facilitation:

  • Full refund is granted if cancellation is requested within 48 hours of registration and prior to document submission to the college.
  • Requests must be raised via email to support@skillora.in with application reference ID.
  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment source within 5 to 7 working days.

4. Dispute Resolution & Support

If you encounter any discrepancies regarding college fee claims or seat receipts, our student assistance desk investigates and mediates directly with the college management to ensure fair resolution.
