Privacy Policy

How Skillora India collects, protects, and handles student, candidate, and institutional partner data.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Commitment to Privacy

Skillora India ("Skillora", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website skilloraindia.com. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of students, job seekers, academic counselors, training partners, and institutional employers across healthcare and hospitality sectors.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information necessary to deliver transparent admissions guidance and verified career placements:

  • Student & Candidate Information: Name, mobile number, email address, educational qualification, preferred program or course, and resume documents uploaded voluntarily.
  • Institutional Partner Data: Official college/hospital name, authorized officer contact details, official recognition details, and approved program intake.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Anonymized device type, browser, IP address, and application timestamps for security and audit integrity.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To connect students directly with verified college counseling desks for admission assistance.
  • To submit job applications and resumes directly to verified hospital and hospitality employers.
  • To provide SMS/WhatsApp counseling alerts and transaction receipts (with option to opt out).
  • To maintain security audit trails and prevent fraudulent or duplicate applications.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade student or candidate phone numbers to third-party telemarketers.

4. Data Storage & Security

All sensitive information, including passwords and cryptographic access tokens, is hashed using SHA-256 protocols. Application data is stored on secure cloud database servers with role-based access restrictions. Only authorized Skillora counselors and verified admissions coordinators can view applicant details.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to your submitted data, request corrections to your application profile, or ask for complete deletion of your resume and account from our servers. To exercise your rights, email our data protection desk at support@skillora.in with subject "Data Request".
